# Arya Bank — Unauthorised Transactions, Disputes and Grievance Redressal

Document ID: DIS-005 · Effective: 1 April 2026

> Arya Bank is a fictional bank created for this evaluation project. All figures are illustrative.

## 1. Reporting an unauthorised transaction

Customers should report any unauthorised electronic transaction as soon as possible through:

- The Arya mobile app (Help → Report fraud)
- Net banking
- The 24x7 helpline: 1800-200-4455
- Email: report.fraud@aryabank.example
- Any Arya Bank branch

The bank will acknowledge the complaint immediately with a complaint reference number.

## 2. Customer liability

A customer's liability for unauthorised electronic transactions depends on when the transaction is reported:

| Reporting time (after receiving the bank's communication) | Customer liability |
|---|---|
| Within 3 working days | Zero liability |
| Within 4 to 7 working days | Limited liability — the lower of the transaction value or ₹10,000 (savings accounts) / ₹25,000 (credit cards with limit above ₹5 lakh) |
| Beyond 7 working days | As per the bank's Board-approved policy |

Customers have zero liability in all cases where the fraud occurred due to negligence or a deficiency on the bank's part, regardless of when it is reported.

If the customer's negligence (such as sharing an OTP, PIN or password) caused the loss, the customer bears the entire loss until the transaction is reported to the bank.

## 3. Shadow credit and resolution timelines

- The bank will credit the disputed amount to the customer's account (shadow credit) within 10 working days of the complaint being reported.
- The complaint will be resolved, and liability established, within 90 days of the date of receipt of the complaint.

## 4. Card transaction disputes (non-fraud)

For disputes such as a merchant not delivering goods, double charges or incorrect amounts, customers can raise a chargeback request within 45 days of the transaction date. The bank will update the customer on the status within 30 days.

## 5. Grievance redressal

1. **Level 1**: Raise the complaint through any channel listed above. Most complaints are resolved within 7 working days.
2. **Level 2**: If unresolved within 14 days or the customer is unhappy with the resolution, escalate to the Principal Nodal Officer at nodal.officer@aryabank.example.
3. **Level 3**: If the complaint is not resolved within 30 days of lodging it with the bank, or the customer is not satisfied with the response, the customer may approach the RBI Ombudsman under the Reserve Bank – Integrated Ombudsman Scheme through cms.rbi.org.in.
