# Arya Bank — KYC (Know Your Customer) Policy

Document ID: KYC-004 · Effective: 1 April 2026

> Arya Bank is a fictional bank created for this evaluation project. All figures are illustrative.

## 1. Purpose

Arya Bank verifies the identity and address of every customer before opening an account, in line with anti-money-laundering (AML) regulations. KYC protects customers and the bank against identity theft, fraud and financial crime.

## 2. Officially Valid Documents (OVDs)

Any one of the following is accepted as proof of identity and address:

- Aadhaar card (with masked Aadhaar number where required)
- Passport
- Driving licence
- Voter ID card
- NREGA job card signed by a State Government officer

A PAN card (or Form 60 if the customer does not have a PAN) is mandatory in addition to an OVD.

## 3. Ways to complete KYC

- **In-branch KYC**: visit any Arya Bank branch with original documents.
- **Video KYC (V-CIP)**: available 9 AM to 9 PM on all days except national holidays. The customer needs their original PAN card, Aadhaar-linked mobile number, a white sheet of paper and a pen for a live signature.
- **Aadhaar OTP-based e-KYC**: allows instant account opening, but such accounts are limited to a maximum balance of ₹1,00,000 and must be converted to full KYC within 12 months, failing which the account will be closed.

## 4. Periodic re-KYC

KYC details must be updated periodically based on the customer's risk category:

| Risk category | Re-KYC frequency |
|---|---|
| High risk | Every 2 years |
| Medium risk | Every 8 years |
| Low risk | Every 10 years |

If there is no change in KYC information, low-risk customers may submit a self-declaration through net banking, the mobile app, email or a letter.

## 5. Non-compliance

If periodic KYC is not updated by the due date, the bank will send at least three reminders, one of which will be by letter. If KYC remains pending, transactions on the account may be partially restricted (credits allowed, debits restricted) until KYC is updated. The account is not closed solely for pending re-KYC.

## 6. Data protection

Customer KYC data is stored in encrypted form and is accessible only to authorised staff. Arya Bank will never ask customers to share their OTP, PIN, CVV or net banking password over phone, SMS, email or social media for KYC purposes.
