# Arya Bank — Savings Account Terms

Document ID: SAV-001 · Effective: 1 April 2026

> Arya Bank is a fictional bank created for this evaluation project. All figures are illustrative.

## 1. Account variants

Arya Bank offers three savings account variants:

| Variant | Minimum Average Monthly Balance (AMB) | Interest rate (per annum) |
|---|---|---|
| Arya Basic | No minimum balance | 2.75% |
| Arya Classic | ₹10,000 (metro/urban), ₹5,000 (semi-urban/rural) | 3.25% |
| Arya Premium | ₹1,00,000 | 3.50% on balances up to ₹10 lakh; 4.00% on the portion above ₹10 lakh |

Interest is calculated on the daily closing balance and credited to the account quarterly, at the end of June, September, December and March.

## 2. Non-maintenance charges

If the Average Monthly Balance falls below the required minimum, a non-maintenance charge applies:

- Arya Classic: 6% of the shortfall, capped at ₹500 per month (plus GST).
- Arya Premium: 6% of the shortfall, capped at ₹600 per month (plus GST).
- Arya Basic: no non-maintenance charge.

Customers are notified by SMS and email when their balance falls below the minimum, and are given one month to restore it before any charge is levied.

## 3. ATM and cash transactions

- Arya Bank ATMs: unlimited free transactions for all variants.
- Other banks' ATMs: 5 free transactions per month (financial and non-financial combined) in non-metro locations and 3 free transactions in the six metro cities. Beyond the free limit, ₹23 per financial transaction and ₹10 per non-financial transaction (plus GST) apply.
- Cash deposits at branches: 4 free cash transactions per month; ₹150 per transaction thereafter.

## 4. Account closure

- Closure within 14 days of opening: free.
- Closure after 14 days and within 12 months of opening: ₹500 charge.
- Closure after 12 months: free.

## 5. Dormant and inoperative accounts

An account with no customer-initiated transactions for 24 consecutive months is classified as **inoperative**. Interest continues to be credited to inoperative accounts. To reactivate, the customer must submit updated KYC documents at any branch or through video KYC; no reactivation fee is charged.
