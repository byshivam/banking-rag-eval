# Arya Bank — Credit Card Fees and Charges

Document ID: CC-002 · Effective: 1 April 2026

> Arya Bank is a fictional bank created for this evaluation project. All figures are illustrative.

## 1. Card variants and annual fees

| Card | Joining fee | Annual fee | Annual fee waiver |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arya Spark | Nil | ₹499 | Waived if annual spend exceeds ₹1,00,000 |
| Arya Voyager | ₹2,500 | ₹2,500 | Waived if annual spend exceeds ₹3,00,000 |
| Arya Signature | ₹10,000 | ₹10,000 | No waiver |

All fees are subject to GST at 18%.

## 2. Interest and payment terms

- Interest-free period: up to 50 days from the transaction date, provided the previous statement's total amount due was paid in full by the due date.
- Finance charge on revolving balances: 3.6% per month (43.2% per annum).
- Minimum amount due: 5% of the total outstanding or ₹200, whichever is higher, plus any EMI amounts and overlimit amount.
- Payment due date: 18 days after the statement date.

## 3. Late payment charges

Late payment charges apply if the minimum amount due is not paid by the due date:

| Total amount due | Late payment charge |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹500 | Nil |
| ₹501 – ₹5,000 | ₹500 |
| ₹5,001 – ₹20,000 | ₹950 |
| Above ₹20,000 | ₹1,300 |

## 4. Other charges

- Cash advance fee: 2.5% of the amount withdrawn, minimum ₹500. Interest is charged on cash advances from the date of withdrawal, with no interest-free period.
- Foreign currency transaction markup: 3.5% for Arya Spark and Arya Voyager; 2% for Arya Signature.
- Overlimit fee: 2.5% of the overlimit amount, minimum ₹550.
- Card replacement (lost, stolen or damaged): ₹100.

## 5. Rewards

- Arya Spark: 1 reward point per ₹150 spent.
- Arya Voyager: 2 reward points per ₹150 spent; 5 points per ₹150 on travel bookings.
- Arya Signature: 4 reward points per ₹150 spent.

Reward points are not earned on fuel, rent payments, wallet loads or cash advances. Points expire 36 months after they are earned. One reward point is worth ₹0.25 when redeemed against the statement balance.

## 6. Lost or stolen cards

Customers must report a lost or stolen card immediately through the Arya mobile app, net banking, or the 24x7 helpline at 1800-200-4455. Once reported, the card is blocked instantly and the customer carries zero liability for any fraudulent transactions made after reporting.
