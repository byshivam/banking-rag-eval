# Arya Bank — Home Loan Policy

Document ID: HL-003 · Effective: 1 April 2026

> Arya Bank is a fictional bank created for this evaluation project. All figures are illustrative.

## 1. Eligibility

- Age: 21 to 60 years at the time of application for salaried applicants; 25 to 65 years for self-employed applicants. The loan must be fully repaid before the applicant turns 70.
- Minimum net monthly income: ₹25,000 for salaried applicants.
- Minimum work experience: 2 years of total experience, including at least 6 months with the current employer.
- Self-employed applicants must have at least 3 years of continuous business vintage and income tax returns for the last 2 years.
- Minimum credit score: 700 (CIBIL or equivalent).

## 2. Loan amount and tenure

- Loan-to-value (LTV) ratio:
  - Up to 90% of the property value for loans up to ₹30 lakh.
  - Up to 80% for loans between ₹30 lakh and ₹75 lakh.
  - Up to 75% for loans above ₹75 lakh.
- Maximum tenure: 30 years.
- EMI should not exceed 50% of the applicant's net monthly income, including existing EMIs.

## 3. Interest rates

Home loans are offered on a floating rate linked to the Repo Rate.

| Credit score | Interest rate (per annum) |
|---|---|
| 800 and above | 8.40% |
| 750 – 799 | 8.65% |
| 700 – 749 | 9.10% |

Women applicants (sole or primary co-applicant) receive a concession of 0.05% on the applicable rate.

## 4. Fees and charges

- Processing fee: 0.50% of the loan amount, minimum ₹5,000 and maximum ₹15,000 (plus GST).
- Prepayment and foreclosure on floating-rate loans taken by individuals: Nil.
- Late EMI payment: penal charge of 2% per month on the overdue EMI amount.

## 5. Documents required

- Identity and address proof (Aadhaar, PAN, passport or voter ID).
- Income proof: last 3 months' salary slips and 6 months' bank statements (salaried), or last 2 years' ITR with computation of income (self-employed).
- Property documents: sale agreement, title deed, approved building plan and NOC from the builder or society.

## 6. Processing time

Sanction decisions are communicated within 7 working days of receiving a complete application. Disbursement takes place after legal and technical verification of the property, typically within 15 working days of sanction.
